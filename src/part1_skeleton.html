POSSUM / SPICE-RACS shown–
In combined mode, SPICE-RACS DR2 components are shown only on sky >0.4° from every POSSUM component, so each Voronoi cell belongs to exactly one survey. Blink mode compares the full catalogues over the whole sky. Click any cell to see its source catalogue.

Import catalogue ▶
Load your own catalogue as CSV/TSV (e.g. a 6dFGS or eROSITA export). Needs columns for position (RA/Dec or l/b, degrees); numeric columns become filter sliders.
Export ▶
PNG/PDF capture the current view with graticules, overlays, labels and colour bar. CSV writes the filtered component list (up to 400k rows).
